ArcGIS Technical Trainer
Senior
Full-time
Hyderabad, India
Training
Posted 2 weeks ago
About This Role

We are looking for an experienced ArcGIS Technical Trainer to deliver high-impact technical training programs on ArcGIS Pro and ArcGIS Enterprise. In this role you will design and develop training materials, mentor GIS professionals, provide guidance on enterprise GIS architecture, and collaborate with technical teams to align training content with industry standards and best practices. This is a full-time, work-from-office role based in Hyderabad with shift timings of 12:30 PM to 9:30 PM and a 6-day work week.

Requirements
Deliver technical training programs and workshops on ArcGIS Pro and ArcGIS Enterprise
Design and develop training materials, curricula, and hands-on labs
Mentor and upskill GIS professionals across teams
Provide guidance on enterprise GIS architecture and deployment best practices
Collaborate with technical teams to align training content with industry standards
Qualifications
4-9 years of professional experience in GIS
Proven track record delivering technical training, workshops, or mentoring programs
Expertise in enterprise GIS deployments and administration
Knowledge of cloud-based deployments and enterprise IT security frameworks
Familiarity with ArcGIS GeoEvent Server, GeoAnalytics Server, and Image Server
ArcGIS Enterprise and ArcGIS Pro certifications highly preferred
Strong communication and presentation abilities
Comfortable with a work-from-office role on a 12:30 PM to 9:30 PM shift, 6-day work week
What We Offer
Work with cutting-edge enterprise GIS technology (ArcGIS Pro and Enterprise)
Shape the skills of GIS professionals across the organisation
Certification support and continuous learning opportunities
Collaborative environment alongside experienced technical teams
Clear career progression and mentorship pathways
Be part of a global geospatial technology leader
