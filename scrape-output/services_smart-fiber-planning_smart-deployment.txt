Why Smart Deployment?

Compress survey-to-design timelines with drone-based aerial surveys, digital twin models of the network, and automated feasibility analysis ,deploying fiber faster, safer, and with fewer surprises in the field.

High-resolution drone aerial surveys with photogrammetry
Digital twin of physical assets for design validation
Automated feasibility analysis on terrain, permits, and obstacles
As-built documentation captured during deployment
Reduced field re-visits through pre-validated designs
