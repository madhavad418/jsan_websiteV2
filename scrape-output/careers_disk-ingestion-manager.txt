Disk Ingestion Manager - Data Center and Networking
Senior
Full-time
Hyderabad, India
Data Center & Networking
Posted 1 week ago
About This Role

We are seeking an experienced Manager / Senior Manager (Disk Ingestion) for our Data Center and Networking operations in Hyderabad. This is a leadership role for someone who has individually managed teams of at least 15 members and brings strong hands-on expertise in data center infrastructure, networking, and large-scale data ingestion. You will own disk ingestion workflows end to end, oversee high-speed data transfer operations, and ensure the reliability, performance, and security of storage and networking systems. This is a full-time, permanent, work-from-office role based in Hyderabad with shift timings of 12:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST. Notice period: Immediate to 30 days (immediate joiners preferred). Budget: as per market standards.

Requirements
Manager / Senior Manager level professional who has individually handled a team of at least 15+ members
Own and manage disk ingestion workflows and large-scale data handling operations end to end
Oversee data center infrastructure including servers, racks, and storage systems
Lead high-speed data transfer operations and ensure data integrity and throughput
Drive performance, reliability, and security across storage and networking systems
Mentor, allocate, and manage the team while coordinating with cross-functional stakeholders
Qualifications
7+ years of overall experience (required on priority)
Mandatory experience in Data Center and Networking
Strong understanding of disk ingestion workflows and large-scale data handling
Hands-on experience with data centre infrastructure (servers, racks, storage systems)
Solid knowledge of networking concepts (TCP/IP, routing, switching, VLANs, VPNs)
Experience with high-speed data transfer tools (Aspera, Signiant, Rsync, FTP/SFTP)
Familiarity with SAN/NAS/object storage systems
Understanding of Linux/Unix systems and scripting basics
Knowledge of RAID configurations, file systems, and backup strategies
Proven people-management experience leading 15+ team members individually
Comfortable with a work-from-office role on a 12:30 PM to 9:30 PM IST shift
Notice period: Immediate to 30 days (immediate joiners preferred)
What We Offer
Full-time, permanent leadership role with a global technology organisation
Lead a team of 15+ members in a high-impact data center and networking function
Work with enterprise-grade storage, networking, and high-speed data transfer systems
Competitive compensation as per market standards
Collaborative environment alongside experienced technical teams
Clear career progression and leadership growth pathways
